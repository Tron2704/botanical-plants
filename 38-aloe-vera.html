Type

Information
  • Aloe vera is a succulent plant adapted to dry environments.
  • It forms a rosette of thick, fleshy, lance-shaped leaves.
  • The leaves contain a clear inner gel and a yellow latex layer beneath the outer skin.
  • Aloe vera is widely cultivated in warm and dry regions.
  • The leaf gel is used in many cosmetic and personal-care products.
  • The plant is also used in traditional medicine, although medicinal claims should not be assumed to be clinically proven.
